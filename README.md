### 🚀 Featured Projects

**🛍️ Superstore Sales Dashboard**
- **Problem:** Leadership needed visibility into which regions, segments, and products were actually driving ₹2.30M in sales and ₹286.4K in profit — not just top-line revenue.
- **Approach:** Cleaned and structured raw transactional data (orders, discounts, product IDs, regions) and broke it down by segment, sub-category, region, and time.
- **Key Insights:**
  - The Consumer segment alone accounts for over 50% of total sales (₹1.16M), making it the single highest-leverage segment for retention and upsell efforts.
  - Sales are heavily region-skewed — West (31.6%) and East (29.6%) together generate over 60% of revenue, while South (17%) significantly underperforms, flagging it as an area needing a targeted push.
  - Phones and Chairs are the top revenue-driving sub-categories, but sales by individual Product ID show one SKU generating almost 3x the sales of the next closest — a concentration risk worth flagging to stakeholders.
  - Order-date trends show sharp, recurring sales spikes rather than steady growth, suggesting seasonal or promotion-driven demand rather than organic growth.
- **Outcome:** Turned raw order-level data into a decision-ready view that highlights where revenue is concentrated and where the business is exposed.

**👥 HR Analytics Dashboard**
- **Problem:** HR needed to understand *why* attrition (9.2%, 41 of 446 employees) was happening, not just track the headline number.
- **Approach:** Cleaned employee records and analyzed attrition across department, salary band, gender, age, and tenure to isolate patterns.
- **Key Insights:**
  - Operations accounts for 56% of all attrition (23 of 41 exits) despite being the largest department — meaning attrition risk is disproportionately concentrated there, not evenly spread across the company.
  - Attrition skews heavily male (68% vs. 32% female), a gap large enough to warrant a deeper look into role distribution or department-specific factors.
  - Lower salary bands (0–3 LPA and 3–6 LPA) show the highest attrition volume combined, pointing toward compensation as a likely driver of exits at the entry-to-mid level.
  - The workforce's average tenure (8.17 years) alongside these patterns suggests attrition isn't a "new hire" problem — it's affecting more experienced employees too.
- **Outcome:** Reframed a routine headcount report into an early-warning view HR can use to target retention efforts where they'll matter most — Operations, lower salary bands, and gender-specific gaps.

**🤖 AI Dashboard**
- **Problem:** Complex, technical AI-related data was inaccessible to non-technical stakeholders trying to gauge performance and adoption trends.
- **Approach:** Cleaned and restructured the raw dataset, then identified the metrics that mattered most to decision-makers rather than displaying every available field.
- **Key Insights:** Distilled technical performance data into a small set of business-relevant indicators, making it possible for non-technical stakeholders to spot trends at a glance instead of digging through raw numbers.
- **Outcome:** A dashboard that translates technical AI data into insights decision-makers can actually act on.
